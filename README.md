# RePlate
RePlate — A Food Surplus Redistribution System that connects food donors, NGOs, and volunteers to rescue surplus food and redistribute it to people in need.


🍽️ RePlate

Rescue Food. Reduce Waste. Replate Hope. ♻️

RePlate is a Food Surplus Redistribution System designed to reduce food waste by connecting food donors, NGOs, volunteers, and organizations. The platform helps coordinate the collection and redistribution of surplus food to people who need it.

🌱 About the Project

Large quantities of edible food are wasted every day while many people still struggle to access sufficient meals. RePlate aims to bridge this gap by creating a simple and organized system for identifying surplus food, coordinating its collection, and connecting it with NGOs and community organizations for redistribution.

🎯 Objectives

- Reduce avoidable food waste
- Encourage responsible food donation
- Connect food donors with NGOs and organizations
- Simplify surplus food collection and redistribution
- Encourage community participation and volunteering
- Create a more sustainable and socially responsible food ecosystem

🔄 How RePlate Works

1. Food Surplus Identified
Restaurants, events, households, or other food providers identify safe surplus food.

2. Donation Request
The donor submits information about the available food.

3. NGO / Volunteer Coordination
Partner NGOs or volunteers coordinate the collection.

4. Food Collection
The surplus food is collected and handled responsibly.

5. Redistribution
The collected food is delivered to people or communities in need.

6. Impact Tracking
The system can maintain records of donations, collections, and redistribution to understand the project's social impact.

👥 Key Stakeholders

- Food Donors — Restaurants, events, households, institutions, etc.
- NGOs / Organizations — Coordinate and facilitate redistribution.
- Volunteers — Assist with collection and delivery.
- Beneficiaries — People and communities receiving the food.
- Project Administration — Manages coordination, records, and overall operations.

✨ Key Features

- Food surplus donation management
- NGO and organization coordination
- Volunteer involvement
- Food collection and redistribution tracking
- Donation status management
- Community participation
- Impact and activity records

🌍 Social Impact
